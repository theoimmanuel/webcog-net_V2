WebCog-Net — Prototype Scenarios

Three mock flight-booking interfaces used in the within-subjects study. Open each in its own tab.

Scenario A · Baseline

Calm dark interface

Low cognitive load. Dark background, readable text, no animation or pop-ups. The baseline measurement.

 Scenario B · Static high-load

Bright, cluttered, pop-up heavy

Deliberately overwhelming. Flashing ads, marquees, countdowns, stacking toasts and modal pop-ups. No intervention.

 Scenario C · Adaptive

WebCog-Net closed-loop

Starts identical to B, then autonomously declutters when sustained high load is detected. Includes a monitor HUD with a load slider and manual controls.
Research prototype · simulated cognitive-load trigger (no webcam used in these HTML mocks).
